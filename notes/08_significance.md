# 8. Uncertainty and significance

Generated by `code/significance.py`; do not edit by hand. Three webs: Gulf of Cadiz, Angolan shelf, Mondego estuary.
Tables: `output/stats_*.csv`. Figures: [`figures/10_statistics/`](../figures/10_statistics/README.md).

## What was run

- Correlations: 10,000 bootstrap resamples of the consumer groups for each interval; 10,000 permutations for each p-value.
- Centralization: 1000 random directed graphs per web, same number of nodes and links.
- Removal, random order: 1000 trials per web and rule (the same orders as `compare_webs.py`).
- Removal, targeted orders: a targeted order is a fixed ranking, so it has no trial-to-trial variation except where groups tie on the score. Each was run 200 times with ties broken at random; where no groups tie there is one possible run.
- Targeted against random: Monte Carlo p-value = share of the 1000 random orders at least as extreme (two-sided, ties counted against the targeted order).
- Two statistics per removal curve: R50, and the area under the share-lost curve (0.5 = no secondary extinctions). R50 moves in steps of 1/(number of living groups), so many random orders tie with a targeted order; the area does not have that problem.
- Checked before writing: the correlations, degree centralization and every R50 here equal the values in `compare_webs_*.csv`.
