## What was done
- Whole-web measures of the cleaned web: components, feeding loops, path length, three kinds of clustering, assortativity, and trophic coherence (Johnson et al. 2014).
- The same measures on three null models:
  - **random (ER)**: same number of groups and links
  - **degree-preserving**: links rewired so every group keeps its number of prey and predators
  - **niche model** (Williams & Martinez 2000): the standard food-web null, with the same size and connectance
- Measures that a null fixes by construction are left out.

## Caveats
- ER and degree-preserving webs cannot contain cannibalism, so they are compared with the web minus its self-loops.
- The ER webs have no basal groups, so measures that need trophic chains (omnivory, trophic level, trophic coherence) are not defined for them.
