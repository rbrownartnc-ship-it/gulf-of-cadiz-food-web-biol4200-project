# Distributions of node and link properties

## What was done
- One histogram per property of the 43 groups, and one for the 397 feeding links (cannibalism excluded).
- Number of prey, number of predators and total degree are drawn against the expected histogram of a random (Erdős–Rényi) web with the same number of groups and links.
- Cumulative degree distribution, divided by link density, against an exponential (Camacho et al. 2002).
- Summary statistics: [`cadiz_distribution_stats.csv`](../output/cadiz_distribution_stats.csv).

## Key numbers
- **number of prey (in-degree)**: mean 9.23, median 9.00, SD 6.78, range 0.00–25.00, skewness 0.47; variance/mean 4.98 against 0.78 in a random web of the same size
- **number of predators (out-degree)**: mean 9.23, median 7.00, SD 7.81, range 0.00–27.00, skewness 0.65; variance/mean 6.61 against 0.78 in a random web of the same size
- **total degree**: mean 18.47, median 18.00, SD 8.37, range 4.00–39.00, skewness 0.44; variance/mean 3.80 against 0.78 in a random web of the same size
- **importance as prey (out-strength)**: mean 0.91, median 0.34, SD 1.26, range 0.00–5.13, skewness 1.82
- **trophic level**: mean 3.14, median 3.27, SD 0.91, range 1.00–4.84, skewness −0.70
- **clustering coefficient**: mean 0.57, median 0.57, SD 0.15, range 0.17–0.94, skewness 0.03
- **betweenness**: mean 0.02, median 0.00, SD 0.04, range 0.00–0.18, skewness 3.67
- **omnivory index**: mean 0.23, median 0.20, SD 0.26, range 0.00–1.30, skewness 2.63
- **diet diversity (effective number of prey)**: mean 5.26, median 4.69, SD 3.17, range 1.00–13.30, skewness 0.52
- **link strength (share of predator's diet)**: mean 0.10, median 0.04, SD 0.16, range 0.00–1.00, skewness 3.19
- Link strength: 28% of links are below 0.01 of the predator's diet, and 3% carry at least half of it. Most links are weak.
- A variance/mean far above the random-web value means the degrees are much more uneven than chance: a few groups have many prey or many predators.
